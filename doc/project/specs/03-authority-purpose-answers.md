# authority or purpose Q&A

## Questions by Codex

What authority or purpose does the work loop begin with?

For example:

1. “Develop this particular software according to this high-level vision.”
2. “Maintain and improve these repositories according to their existing direction.”
3. “Build whatever software you judge valuable.”
4. Initially, “build and improve ai-cluster itself until it becomes increasingly autonomous.”
5. Put differently: the system can autonomously decide how best to spend intelligence, but what ultimately defines “best”? Does that value judgment originate from a standing human goal, from the chosen project, or eventually from the system itself?

My second question is closely related: is self-hosting part of the intended path? I can imagine an early, crude version obtaining free model access and using that capacity to implement better discovery, evaluation, orchestration, and recovery in its own codebase. That recursive bootstrapping sounds much closer to the character of your idea than assigning it ordinary backlog tickets.

## Answers

### What authority or purpose does the work loop begin with?

Not sure. It should at least be capable of keeping itself busy, but it's not clear what that means in practice.

I may give it tasks, but the way I work with paid agents/LLMs is limited by myself. The agents usually perform tasks in
minutes, and then I review (without review the results just lack finesse in certain parts). Together with me planning
and producing ideas, this is limited to one project at a time with few times of 2 in parallel. And I have everything
I need to work like that – no demand for long running tasks from my side. This just describes how I work - this tool
shall thus be able to run alone – maybe in future I give it tasks. And I will surely want to check changes it does to
itself for quality.

And if I let it work alone and maybe even interact with other projects (as in dong PRs) then I might not want to be
associated with it unless I review things. But PRs to other projects should not be done during it's early stages.

### Is self-hosting part of the intended path?

If by self-hosting you mean having a small local LLM, then yes. That does not have to be up and runninng all the time -
maybe it should – but 1-4 GB of RAM should be enough. As I said – the fallback which is always available.
